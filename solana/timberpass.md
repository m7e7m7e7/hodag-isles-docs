# TimberPass

The **TimberPass** is the season pass: 30 tiers of rewards, skins and extra quests. You climb the tiers by playing.

## Season 1: First Frost

| | |
|---|---|
| **Tiers** | 30 |
| **Length** | 21 days, from 11 November 2026 |

## Prices

Prices are set in US dollars and paid in **SOL** on Solana at the current rate.

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| **TimberPass** | $5 |
| **Blaze** (the higher pass level) | $10 |
| **Blaze upgrade** (if you already own the TimberPass) | $5 |
| **Tier skip** | $0.50 |

## How paying works

* You pay from the wallet linked to your account, with a normal SOL transfer that you approve in the wallet.
* The price in SOL is held for **15 minutes**.
* Keep a little extra SOL for the network fee (a tiny fraction of a cent).
* The server checks every payment on the Solana chain before it unlocks anything.
* If you send by hand, paste the **transaction signature** (or a [Solscan](https://solscan.io) link) in the game.

{% hint style="info" %}
The free track is open to everyone; the pass adds the paid rewards on top.
{% endhint %}
