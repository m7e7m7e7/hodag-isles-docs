# Feed the Squonk

The **Squonk** sits by the stove, weeping and begging for coins. He apologises for everything, and he knows every secret on the isles.

## How it works

* Talk to the Squonk and pick an amount (**$1, $5, $10, $25, $50** or your own), paid in **SOL** on Solana at the current rate.
* You send it from the wallet linked to your account. The price in SOL is held for 15 minutes, and you need a little extra SOL for the network fee.
* The server checks the payment on the Solana chain. Then the whole room watches the coins fly.
* The Squonk pays you back in **secrets**: hints about the isles.

{% hint style="info" %}
Feeding the Squonk is a donation to support the game. It's optional, and it doesn't buy any advantage in fights.
{% endhint %}
