# Sign In with Your Wallet

You can sign in with a **BNB Chain wallet** or with **X**.

## What signing in does

* Your hero, progress and rewards are saved to your account.
* You can earn **$HODAG** rewards (see [Earn Before Launch](../token/earn.md)).
* You can buy the [TimberPass](timberpass.md) and [feed the Squonk](squonk.md).

## Is it safe?

Signing in with your wallet asks for **one free signature**. It proves the wallet is yours. **It sends nothing and costs no gas.**

{% hint style="danger" %}
Hodag Isles will **never** ask for your seed phrase or private key, and never asks you to send funds to "verify" your wallet. See [Stay Safe](../community/safety.md).
{% endhint %}

## Guest play

You can play as a guest without signing in. Guests can't earn rewards, and their progress stays in that browser only.
