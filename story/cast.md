# Cast of Legends

| Character | Who they are |
|---|---|
| **Sourdough Sam** | The camp cook and your mentor. Loud, warm and proud of his cooking. *"First you feed the stove, then the stove feeds you."* |
| **Mike Fink** | River trader and fisherman at Fink's Landing. "Half horse, half alligator." Brags about every fish. |
| **Johnny Appleseed** | Keeper of the orchard. Barefoot, a tin pot for a hat, talks to trees. Seeds, sprouts and brews. |
| **Johnny Inkslinger** | Paul's bookkeeper. Counts everything, ink to his elbows. He keeps the camp's ledger. |
| **Big Ole** | The camp blacksmith. Huge, few words, a soft heart. Runs the Brawl Pit. |
| **The Bull of the Woods** | The pompous camp boss in the Boss's Lodge. Wants peace with the Hodag at any price. Secretly afraid. |
| **The Squonk** | A sad little critter who weeps, begs for coins and pays you back in secrets. |
| **Babe the Blue Ox** | Hauls the big logs. Doesn't talk. Snorts, stamps and nudges. |
| **The Hidebehind** | The seeker in hide-and-seek. Nobody has ever seen it from the front. |
| **Paul Bunyan** | Gone for help. His giant boots, axe and stove are still in camp. |

## The four heroes

The legend statues let you take up the calling of a legend. See [Choose Your Hero](../play/heroes.md).

| Hero | Legend | Style |
|---|---|---|
| **The Lumberjack** | Paul Bunyan's crew | Double-bit axe and a log shield. Close fighter who can climb the Hodag. |
| **The Wrangler** | Pecos Bill | Bow and a lasso that pulls you forward. |
| **The Sower** | Johnny Appleseed | A crook and seed magic. |
| **The Steel Driver** | John Henry | Hammer and spike. Digs underground and strikes from below. |
