# Getting Started

## 1. Open the game

Hodag Isles runs in your browser on desktop and phone. There's nothing to download. Open the game link (see [Links](../community/links.md)) and press **Play**.

## 2. Play as a guest, or sign in

* **Guest:** jump straight in. Good for a first look.
* **Signed in** with **X** or a **BNB wallet**: your progress, hero and rewards follow your account, and you can earn **$HODAG** rewards. See [Sign In with Your Wallet](../bnb/wallet.md).

## 3. Read the guide

The **How to play** guide opens on your first visit. Press **Esc** any time to open it again.

## 4. Feed the stove

Sourdough Sam hands you your first log. Carry it to the **Big Stove** and throw it in. That's the heart of the game. See [Feed the Big Stove](feed-the-stove.md).

## 5. Pick your hero

New loggers start as the **Lumberjack**. The other heroes unlock after **10 minutes of play or 1 win**. See [Choose Your Hero](heroes.md).

{% hint style="success" %}
Everyone you see on the isles plays in the same camp and feeds the same fire. Say hi in chat with **Enter**.
{% endhint %}
