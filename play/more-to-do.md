# More to Do

## ⚔️ Duels

Challenge other loggers in **Big Ole's Brawl Pit**. Every hero plays differently, so learn the matchups.

## 🎣 Fishing

Cast off **Fink's Landing** with Mike Fink. Collect fish, enter fishing events and watch out for the **Goofang**.

## 🏕️ Crews

Join or found a **crew** (a guild) at the Boss's Lodge and fly your banner.

## 🌳 Stumplings

Carry them, stack them on your head, throw them and go **Stumpling bowling** in the Stump Arena.

## 🏊 Swim and explore

Swim the river and the Big Lake, cross the Community Bridge to the east bank and check the **map** for the places you've been.

## 💃 Hang out

Chat, dance (**G**) by the fire and meet the camp's legends. Read the **Camp Journal** (**J**) to follow the story.
