# Earn Before Launch

You can earn **$HODAG** rewards now, before the token launches. Johnny Inkslinger keeps the ledger: the game counts every reward to your account, and you can see your total in the **Rewards** panel.

{% hint style="info" %}
You need to be signed in with **X** or a **Solana wallet** to earn. Guests can't earn rewards.
{% endhint %}

## Ways to earn

| Action | Reward |
|---|---|
| **Invite a friend** who signs in with X or a wallet | **500** per friend, up to 50 friends |
| **Post about Hodag Isles on X**, once a day | **100** |
| **Whitelist quests** on X: follow, like, reply, quote | A spot on the whitelist |

### Invite friends

Open the **Invite** panel in the game to get your invite link. When a friend arrives through it and signs in, you're credited once for that friend.

### Daily post

Post about the game on X, then paste your post's link in the game. It's checked automatically: the post must be yours and from today.

### Whitelist

Do the quests on the whitelist board, then apply with your Solana wallet address.

## Getting paid

Rewards are paid in **$HODAG** on Solana, to the wallet on your account, after launch.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Connect your Solana wallet before payout so your rewards have somewhere to go.
{% endhint %}
