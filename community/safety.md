# Stay Safe

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Nobody from Hodag Isles will ever ask for your seed phrase or private key.** If someone does, they're the Hodag in a trench coat.
{% endhint %}

* **Signing in** with your wallet is one free signature. It never moves funds.
* **We never DM first** asking you to "verify", "sync" or "claim" by sending tokens.
* **Only use the official links** on the [Links](links.md) page and from [@hodagisles](https://x.com/hodagisles).
* **Check the $HODAG contract** before you buy. The only official one is `0x190e79baf5b53616862df67733ba80e9d4857777`. Copycat tokens will appear.
* **All payments in the game** (TimberPass, the Squonk) are normal BNB transfers you approve yourself in your wallet. Check the amount before you approve.
