# FAQ

<details>

<summary>Is Hodag Isles free?</summary>

Yes. The game is free to play in your browser. The TimberPass and feeding the Squonk are optional.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Do I need a wallet to play?</summary>

No. You can play as a guest. Sign in with X or a BNB wallet to save your progress and earn $HODAG rewards.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Does it work on my phone?</summary>

Yes. Hodag Isles runs in mobile browsers with on-screen controls.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Which chain is it on?</summary>

BNB Smart Chain. The TimberPass and the Squonk take BNB, and $HODAG launches on flap on BNB Chain.

</details>

<details>

<summary>When do I get my $HODAG rewards?</summary>

After the token launches. They're paid on BNB Chain to the wallet on your account. Check your total in the Rewards panel.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why can I only play the Lumberjack?</summary>

New loggers start as the Lumberjack. The Wrangler, Sower and Steel Driver unlock after 10 minutes of play or 1 win.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Is the Hodag real?</summary>

As real as any tall tale. The Hodag is a folk legend from Rhinelander, Wisconsin, first told by loggers in the 1890s.

</details>
